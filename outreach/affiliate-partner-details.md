# Gradlify Affiliate Partner Details

Gradlify is an 11+ platform with free exams, practice, mocks, and resources for Year 4/5 families.

## How It Works

1. We create a tracked partner link/code for you.
2. You share Gradlify with parents.
3. Parents get free 11+ practice and exams.
4. If a family upgrades to a paid plan, the upgrade is tracked back to you.
5. We pay partner commission monthly.

Option B: 20% of the first paid month.

For larger partners, we can discuss custom terms after the first 10 paid conversions.
