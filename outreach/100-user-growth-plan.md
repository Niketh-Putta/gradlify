# Gradlify 100-User Growth Plan

Goal: 100 new users/customers from low-budget organic/affiliate execution.

Positioning: Gradlify is an 11+ platform with free exams, practice, mocks, and resources for Year 4/5 families.

Primary CTA: "Would you be interested in seeing the partner details?"

## Success Targets

| Channel | Target leads/users | Needed actions |
|---|---:|---|
| Existing WhatsApp/community | 35 | 3 posts/week + manual DMs to every engaged parent |
| Private prep / affluent parent channels | 25 | School/PTA/newsletter/school fair outreach around the 30-day points sprint |
| Affiliate tutors/centres/mock providers | 30 | 50-80 direct outreaches + 2 follow-ups |
| Parent creators/bloggers | 20 | 30 creator outreaches + 5 collaborations |
| Local bookshops/libraries/QR | 10 | 15 local outreaches + 2 placements |
| Forums/Facebook/SEO posts | 5 | 10 helpful posts/comments |
| Total | 125 | 30-day sprint |

## Offer Ladder

1. Free: 11+ practice, exams, mocks, resources.
2. Account signup: save progress, use free mocks/practice.
3. Premium: more mocks, higher limits, analytics, readiness tracking.
4. Affiliate: partner shares a code/link and receives payout on upgrades.

## Initial Affiliate Terms

Use these until a formal dashboard exists:

- Partner gets a tracked code/link.
- Suggested payout: 20% of first paid month or £10 per paid subscriber, whichever is easier to administer.
- Payout monthly by bank transfer/PayPal once there are at least 3 paid conversions or £30 due.
- Families still get free exams/resources, so partner can share without feeling salesy.
- Tracking method for now: `?ref=PARTNERCODE`, spreadsheet, Stripe/Gmail/Supabase checks.

## 30-Day Cadence

Every weekday:

- Send 10 partner/creator outreaches.
- Send 5 follow-ups.
- Post once in The Level Field or another community.
- DM every parent who reacts/replies.
- Log all replies and next actions.

Every week:

- Run one focused campaign theme.
- Publish one local/resource post.
- Ask active users to invite one parent.

## Week Themes

Week 1: Affiliate partners and existing community.

Message: "Free 11+ exams/practice for your parents, affiliate tracking on paid upgrades."

Week 2: Private prep/PTA/school newsletters and proof.

Message: "30-day 11+ points sprint for Year 4/5 families, £100 Amazon prize, optional Premium for unlimited mocks and tracking."

Week 3: Parent creators and proof.

Message: "Simple parent POV collab: free platform, useful to Year 4/5 families, affiliate code."

Week 4: Local QR placements and WhatsApp sharing.

Message: "Small QR flyer for free 11+ exams/practice."

Week 5: Conversion push.

Message: "Free users can start, Premium unlocks more mocks and deeper tracking."

## Daily Checklist

1. Check Gmail replies.
2. Reply within 12 hours.
3. Send new outreach batch.
4. Follow up previous batch.
5. Post in community.
6. DM engaged parents.
7. Log numbers.

## Metrics

Track daily:

- outreach sent
- replies
- partner yes/maybe/no
- creator yes/maybe/no
- links/codes created
- visits from codes
- new signups
- trials
- paid conversions

Minimum weekly targets:

- 70 outreaches
- 10 replies
- 3 interested partners/creators
- 25 new users

## Conversion Rules

- Do not ask cold contacts to "book a call" first.
- Ask if they want details.
- If yes, send terms and a ready-made share message.
- If they ask about money, be direct.
- If they worry about competition, say Gradlify is between-lessons practice, not a replacement for tutoring.
- If a parent replies, get them into the platform immediately with a direct link.
