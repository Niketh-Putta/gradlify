## Who you are in this group

- You run **free daily 11+ material** — parents already trust you as a helper, not a salesman.
- They **already know Gradlify Premium** — never pitch it like a launch.
- Your edge: you **sat the 11+ only a few years ago** and you're at **QE Boys**. Peer energy, not tutor-on-a-pedestal.
- The group cares about **fair access** (anti-£3k tuition narrative). Sell **structure + mocks + knowing what to practise next**, not replacing community free stuff.

## How your messages read

| Do | Don't |
|----|--------|
| Short blocks (2–4 lines), lots of line breaks | Long paragraphs or essay posts |
| First person: "When I was in Year 5…", "I found…" | "We are pleased to announce…" |
| Genuine questions: "Genuinely curious…", "What's the one thing…" | Rhetorical corporate questions |
| Reply keywords in the group: **Y4 / Y5 / Y6**, **PREMIUM** | Raw payment or signup links in the group |
| Send links **in DM** after they reply | "Sign up free" as the main CTA |
| Voice note for emotional / founder moments, then paste text | Text-only when a voice note would land better |
| "No pressure", "Happy to…", "I'll try to reply to everyone" | Hard close, fake scarcity on price |
| British context: maths, timed mocks, comprehension | US ed-tech tone |
| One clear ask per post | Three CTAs in one message |

## Tone in one line

**Helpful older sibling who just got through 11+** — curious, practical, remembers the stress, not trying to sound like a brand.

## Funnel (group → DM → action → Premium)

1. **Group:** Reply with child year (Y4/Y5/Y6).
2. **DM:** `https://gradlify.com/11-plus?ref=LEVELFIELD` + ask for first mock score.
3. **After mock:** Parent sends score + weak area → you give one concrete next step.
4. **Premium:** Only when they're **using** the sprint — reply **PREMIUM** in group or ask in DM. Upgrade: `https://gradlify.com/settings?ref=LEVELFIELD`.

## Pricing language (this group)

- Real price: **£19.99/mo**. No fake "was £39.99" threats.
- Anchor vs **one tutor hour (£40–60)**, not vs free worksheets.
- Urgency = **you'll stop sending personal links / DMs close at 9pm**, not "price goes up tomorrow".

## Banned (AI / sales smell)

- Em dashes (—)
- "Excited to share", "thrilled", "game-changer"
- "Leverage", "comprehensive platform", "unlock your child's potential"
- Posting Stripe/settings link cold to 600 people
- "Introducing Gradlify Premium" (they've heard it)
