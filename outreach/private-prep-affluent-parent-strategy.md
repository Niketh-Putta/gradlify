# Private Prep / Affluent Parent Strategy

Principle: "Sell to the rich until you can sell to everyone else."

For Gradlify, this means the paid campaign should not mainly chase parents who want free 11+ resources. It should target parents who already spend on education: independent prep families, selective senior-school applicants, high-income London/Bucks/Sutton parents, and PTA/school-fair ecosystems.

## Positioning Shift

Do not position Gradlify as:

- "free 11+ resources"
- "affordable alternative to tutors"
- "help for families who cannot pay"

That positioning is good for the mission, but it attracts low-intent free users.

For private prep parents, position Gradlify as:

- "competitive 11+ mock practice"
- "extra reps between tutoring and school"
- "leaderboard sprint with a £100 prize"
- "unlimited mock attempts and progress tracking for serious children"
- "a way for parents to see whether their child is actually improving"

## Best Paid Segment

Primary segment:

- Year 4 and Year 5 pupils at independent prep schools
- Parents targeting selective senior schools, grammar schools, scholarships, or 11+ entry
- Areas: North London, Harrow/Pinner, Sutton/Kingston, Bucks/High Wycombe, Slough/Beaconsfield/Gerrards Cross

Why:

- They already pay school fees, tutors, exam registrations, mocks, clubs, and camps.
- £24.99/month feels small compared with tutoring or school fees.
- They respond to competition, ranking, mock performance, and selective-school language.

## Channel Priority

1. PTA / Friends Association / parent reps
   - Best route because it reaches parents directly.
   - Pitch as a useful 11+ sprint/resource, not a school endorsement.

2. School office / admissions / registrar
   - Ask who handles parent newsletter enrichment/resources.
   - Do not ask them to "promote my app" directly.

3. School fairs / summer fairs / auction prizes
   - Offer a sponsored prize or free Premium month for raffle/auction.
   - This gets Gradlify in front of paying parents with less resistance.

4. Local premium clubs
   - Tennis, cricket, swimming, coding, music, Kumon-style enrichment.
   - Many private prep families overlap with these.

5. Parent creators with affluent London/Bucks audiences
   - Better than general mummy bloggers.
   - Look for private school, prep school, London family lifestyle, HENRY parent content.

## Offer To Schools / PTA

Do not lead with affiliate commission. Schools may dislike commercial kickbacks.

Lead with:

- free for the school
- optional for parents
- only checking interest first before sending details
- free access to the 30-day 11+ points sprint
- £100 Amazon gift card prize
- useful independent practice outside school
- optional parent newsletter blurb
- optional PTA raffle/auction prize

If PTA asks about fundraising:

- offer 20% of first paid month back to PTA, or £10 per paid family, framed as PTA fundraising.

## Offer To Parents

Free:

- join the 30-day points sprint
- complete mock exams
- compete for £100 Amazon gift card

Premium:

- unlimited mocks
- bigger mock sessions
- progress/history tracking
- more attempts to climb the leaderboard
- less guessing about weak areas

## Messaging Psychology

Private prep parents buy when the message hits:

- "My child is bright but needs more exam reps."
- "We already pay for school/tutor, but I need visibility between lessons."
- "I want to know where my child stands."
- "A competitive challenge will motivate my child."
- "This is cheap compared with another tutoring hour."

Avoid guilt-based affordability language with this segment. Use performance, competition, consistency, and visibility.

## 14-Day Implementation

Day 1:

- Build target list of 30 independent prep schools and PTA contacts.
- Prioritise schools with age 3-11/4-11 and explicit 11+ outcomes.

Day 2:

- Send 10 school/registrar emails.
- Send 10 PTA/friends emails where contact exists.

Day 3:

- Send 10 premium club/local enrichment emails.
- Post refined sprint message in The Level Field.

Day 4:

- DM every parent who replies from WhatsApp.
- Create school-specific referral codes.

Day 5:

- Follow up warm replies.
- Offer newsletter blurb, raffle prize, or PTA fundraising version.

Days 6-10:

- Add 10 new targets per day.
- Follow up non-responders after 3 working days.

Days 11-14:

- Push "last chance to join sprint" to parents.
- DM leaderboard/score-active parents with Premium pitch.

## Success Target

From 50 affluent-channel contacts:

- 5-8 replies
- 2-3 newsletter/PTA/school-fair placements
- 60-120 affluent parent visits
- 20-40 signups
- 5-15 paid conversions

This is a better paid-conversion path than broad free-resource posting.

## School Outreach Rule

Never imply the school endorses Gradlify unless they explicitly agree.

Use wording like:

"Would this be suitable to include as an optional parent resource or PTA newsletter item?"

not:

"Can you promote this to your pupils?"

For the first message, also make clear:

"There is no cost to the school. I am only checking whether this may be useful before sending details."
