# Parent Creator Brief

Campaign: Gradlify 11+ platform

## Core Message

Gradlify is an 11+ platform with free exams, practice, mocks, and resources for Year 4/5 families.

## What To Create

One simple parent POV video or post:

- "I found a free 11+ platform for Year 4/5 practice."
- Show the homepage/platform.
- Mention free exams/practice/mocks/resources.
- Tell parents they can try it free.
- Use creator link/code.

## What Not To Say

- Do not promise a pass.
- Do not claim guaranteed school entry.
- Do not say it replaces tutoring.
- Do not say results are instant or guaranteed.

## Suggested Caption

If you’re preparing for the 11+, I found a platform called Gradlify with free exams, practice, mocks, and resources for Year 4/5 families.

Useful if you want extra Maths/English practice at home without starting with another paid course.

Try it here: [LINK]

## Creator Deal

Starting offer:

- Affiliate link/code.
- £10 per paid subscriber or 20% of first paid month.
- Optional small fixed fee for selected creators after reviewing audience fit.
