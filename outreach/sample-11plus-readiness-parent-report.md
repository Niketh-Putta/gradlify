# Sample Parent Report: 11+ Readiness Check

Student: Sample Year 5 pupil  
Target area: Barnet / North London selective schools  
Completion time: 12 minutes

## Overall Readiness

Readiness band: Developing  
Current signal: The student has enough foundation knowledge to benefit from focused exam practice, but the report suggests weak timing and vocabulary precision are limiting performance.

## Strongest Area

Maths arithmetic accuracy  
The student answered most direct arithmetic questions correctly and showed secure understanding of core operations. This is a good base for multi-step 11+ problem solving.

## Weakest Area

Vocabulary in context  
The student struggled when a word had to be inferred from the surrounding sentence rather than recognised directly. This is a common issue in GL-style English and comprehension papers.

## Timing Signal

The student spent too long on the first half of the check and then rushed the final questions. This suggests they may know more than their score shows, but need timed repetition and question-skipping strategy.

## Recommended Next 7 Days

1. Complete three 10-minute vocabulary-in-context sets.
2. Complete two timed comprehension mini-sections using passage-first reading.
3. Complete one mixed maths speed set to maintain arithmetic fluency.
4. Retake a short readiness check after seven days and compare weakest area movement.

## Parent Summary

The priority is not more random worksheets. The next improvement should come from targeted vocabulary-in-context practice and timed comprehension habits. Maths should be maintained, not ignored, but English precision is the bigger risk area right now.
