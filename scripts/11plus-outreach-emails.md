# Gradlify outreach (short, affiliate-style ask)

**Goal:** Introduce yourself, show you follow their work, ask if they are open to a quick call about an affiliate-style paid promo. No long pitch.

**Do not mention:** fee amounts, product features, or your age in the first message.

**Subject ideas (pick one):**
- Quick call about a collab?
- Affiliate-style promo with Gradlify?
- Open to a 10 min chat?

**Call line (default):** Would you be open to a quick call (about 10 minutes) to see if it could work for your audience? Happy to work around your schedule.

"Great, thank you. I'm flexible this week. Would any of these work (UK time): [day/time], [day/time], [day/time]? Or send a time that suits you and I'll make it work."

On the call (10 min): who you are, what Gradlify is in one sentence, affiliate idea (they share link, earn on sign-ups), ask what would work for them.
