# Gradlify — Do This In Order
**One list. Top to bottom. Don't skip.**

**Psychology rule:** Parents buy **proof** (mock score), not apps. Partners say **yes on a call**, not in email. Email = book the call. Call = close the deal.

**Voice-first rule:** Every warm partner gets a **10-minute phone/Zoom call** before you send links or expect a post. Text is for scheduling and follow-up only.

---

## BLOCK 1 — Ship (do before any big promotion)

*Why: If you send 100 parents to a broken funnel, you waste trust forever.*

1. **Set production env** `VITE_APP_TRACK=11PLUS` → redeploy → test signup: parent must see **schools + GL/CEM**, not GCSE grades.

2. **Fix mock price everywhere** → **£10** (not £9.99) on Stripe, live mock page, and every post.

3. **Add signup CTA** on 11+ landing nav → button: **“Start free practice”** → goes to signup.

4. **Add `?ref=` line** on landing → when URL has `?ref=PRLC`, show: **“Recommended by Pinner Road Learning Centre”** (swap name per partner).

5. **Ship post-mock parent screen** (minimum version):
   - Show **score** (e.g. 34/50)
   - Show **3 weak topics** by name
   - One button: **“Practice weak topics free”**
   - One line: **“Unlimited mocks + full tracking — £19.99/mo, 3-day trial”**

6. **Fix copy lies** → if product allows 1 mock/day, never say “2 mocks/day” anywhere.

7. **Test the full path yourself** (30 min):
   - New email → signup → sit 1 short mock → see score screen → click upgrade
   - If any step fails, fix before Block 2.

### Step 9 — On every call, say this (10 minutes)

**Minute 0–1 — Their world (psychology: you're listening, not selling)**
> “What do your parents struggle with most right now — timing, English comp, or knowing if they're on track?”

**Minute 1–3 — One sentence product**
> “Gradlify is extra timed practice and mocks **between** your lessons — not replacing you. Parents start free, see a score and weak topics, then some upgrade to £19.99/mo.”

**Minute 3–5 — Screen share or send link**
> “Can you open gradlify.com/11-plus on your phone? I'll walk you through one mock.”

**Minute 5–7 — The offer (only after they nod)**
> “Simplest deal: you share a tracked link. You get **30% of every payment** from families who sign up through you. I track it, pay monthly. No upfront cost for you.”

**Minute 7–9 — Close**
> “If that works, I'll send your link today and a one-line blurb you can paste to parents. We also have a **live group mock on 14 June** — want to mention it to your list?”

**Minute 9–10 — Next step**
> “I'll email your link and caption within an hour. Shout if you want a co-branded mock in July.”
