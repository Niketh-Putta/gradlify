## Partner / affiliate threads

| They said… | Intent | Your move |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| Interested, wants details | `book_call` | Short product line + two call times. Deck only if they asked for slides/brief. |
| Asked for deck / slides / overview | `deck_then_call` | Say you'll attach deck (Niketh attaches manually) + push call with two times. |
| Asked for trial / access | `trial_then_call` | Signup link + "reply with email you used" + offer call to walk through. |
| Wants call / sent times | `confirm_call` | Confirm time, send Meet link, one sentence agenda. |
| Said yes to 30% / deal | `send_link` | Partner URL `?ref=CODE`, 3-sentence caption, mention 14 June mock. |
| Login broken | `fix_login` | Ask for exact error or offer call to fix live today. |
| No commission (like Tina) | `polite_close` | Thank them, leave door open, no chase. |
| Stop contacting | `archive` | Do not reply. Tracker: do_not_contact. |
| Silence 7+ days after your last | `one_nudge` | One line + two call times. No guilt trip. |
